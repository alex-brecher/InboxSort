## Detailed Description

**Finally, the sorting Gmail should have had from the start.**

Gmail gives you labels, stars, and categories -- but no way to simply sort your inbox by sender name, or push unread messages to the top. InboxSort fixes that with one-click sorting that lives right inside Gmail.

**SORT YOUR WAY**

- **Oldest First** -- Surface buried emails you may have missed
- **Newest First** -- Restore Gmail's default order with one click
- **Sender A-Z / Z-A** -- Group conversations by who sent them, alphabetically
- **Unread First** -- Push unread messages to the top so nothing slips through

**GROUP BY SENDER**

Turn on the Group overlay to visually cluster emails from the same sender -- complete with color-coded badges and email counts. Works alongside any sort mode, so you can sort by date and still see sender groups at a glance.

**FILTER AND FIND IN SECONDS**

- **Quick Filters** -- Toggle to show only starred, unread, or emails with attachments
- **Live Search** -- Type in the filter bar to instantly narrow your inbox by sender, subject, or snippet
- **Bulk Select** -- Select all visible filtered emails with one click for batch actions

**LIVE STATS BAR**

A slim stats bar sits below your sort tabs showing real-time counts: total emails, unread, starred, and attachments on the current page. Click any stat to toggle it as a filter.

**KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS**

Power through your inbox without touching the mouse:
- Alt+1 through Alt+5: Switch sort modes instantly
- Alt+6: Toggle Group by Sender
- Alt+0: Clear all filters
- / (slash): Jump to the filter search bar
- ?: Open the keyboard shortcut cheat sheet

**SETTINGS THAT ADAPT TO YOU**

- **Auto-sort on load** -- Your preferred sort applies the moment Gmail opens
- **Remember per label** -- Set different sort orders for Inbox, Sent, Promotions, or any label
- **Pause Sorting** -- Snooze sorting for 15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour when you need a break
- **Customizable tabs** -- Show or hide individual sort tabs to keep the toolbar clean
- **Accent color** -- Choose from six colors (blue, green, purple, red, orange, teal) to match your style
- **Backup & Restore** -- Export your settings to JSON and import them on another machine

**WORKS WITH DARK MODE**

InboxSort automatically detects Gmail's dark theme and adjusts all colors for comfortable viewing -- no configuration needed.

**HOW IT WORKS**

InboxSort adds a small toolbar directly below Gmail's action bar. Click a tab to sort, use the search bar to filter, and glance at the stats bar for a snapshot of your page. Everything happens visually using CSS transforms -- your actual inbox is never modified, and no emails are moved, deleted, or changed in any way.

**PRIVACY FIRST**

- No data leaves your browser. Ever.
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry
- No external servers or API calls
- No access to email content beyond what is visible on screen
- All preferences are stored locally using Chrome's built-in storage
- Open source and fully auditable

**WORKS ACROSS GMAIL**

InboxSort works on every Gmail view: Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam, Trash, custom labels, and categories like Promotions and Social. It handles pagination seamlessly and re-applies your sort whenever you navigate between pages or labels.

Install InboxSort and take control of your Gmail inbox in under 5 seconds.
